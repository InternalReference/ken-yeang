### K Tower — no image exists in reach

No published render or photograph of K Tower, Kuwait City could be retrieved. ResearchGate returns 403, the International Design Awards page does not expose the project image, the Architectural Review article is text-only, and the practice's own portfolio does not list it.

**Resolution:** the deck carries an **original diagram drawn by External Reference**, based on the Architectural Review's written description of the scheme, and labelled as such on the slide. It is not the architect's drawing and must never be presented as one.

### `/solaris` — expanded 19 Aug 2026
Seven further images recovered from the practice's own **archived** website via web.archive.org (`trhamzahyeang.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/02/Solaris-*.jpg`), all 1600×1000. © T.R. Hamzah & Yeang. Plus `solaris-1.jpg` from e-architect. All eight verified visually as Solaris.

### K Tower / Al Asima, Kuwait — exhaustively searched, nothing found
Routes tried and failed: ResearchGate (403), International Design Awards (no project image in markup), The Architectural Review via TheFreeLibrary (blocked), the practice's current site (project not listed), the practice's **archived** site on web.archive.org across both site generations — the old `/asima/` directory holds only sliced table-layout fragments and the `img-asm/` originals were never captured, and a general web search.

Note for anyone continuing: the built **Assima Tower** in Kuwait City (302 m, 65 floors, 2023) is by **PLP Architecture**, not Yeang. Yeang's Al Asima scheme was an earlier, unbuilt proposal, and the two must not be confused.
**Outcome:** the K Tower slide was removed from the deck on 19 Aug 2026, since every project slide must carry a real image and none exists. K Tower remains listed in the Works index as an unbuilt Kuwait City project, and the design strategies it demonstrates are carried in chapter 3 as text.
