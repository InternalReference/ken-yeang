## Two things to know before quoting any of it

1. **No measured performance data exists for any Yeang building.** Design-stage simulation exists, and two independent Aga Khan reviews exist, but no metered post-occupancy energy figure and no whole-life carbon assessment has ever been published. Treat any performance claim as unproven.
2. **He worked almost entirely in the humid tropics.** His only hot-arid project, a Kuwait City tower, was never built. Chapter 3 is our translation, not his.

## Where to research further

- **archnet.org** — the Aga Khan archive. The best source, and the only place with independent review reports.
- **web.archive.org** — the old `trhamzahyeang.com` holds far more project material than the practice's current site.
- **skyscrapercenter.com** — verified heights, dates and floor counts.
